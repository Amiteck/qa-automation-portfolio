Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how refund requests are handled for the downloadable Playwright QA Automation Portfolio Project.

1. Digital product

The Playwright QA Automation Portfolio Project is a downloadable digital product. Access to the product files may be provided immediately after a successful purchase.

2. Change-of-mind refunds

Because digital content can be accessed or downloaded immediately, change-of-mind refunds are generally not offered after access has been provided, except where required by applicable law or mandatory consumer rights.

3. Product or delivery problems

If you cannot access the purchased files, receive the wrong product, or believe the product is materially different from its description, contact us within 14 days of purchase. We will first try to resolve the issue through replacement access, corrected files, or technical support. Where the issue cannot reasonably be resolved, a refund may be considered.

4. Duplicate or unauthorized purchases

If you believe you were charged more than once or a transaction was unauthorized, contact us promptly so the transaction can be investigated with the payment provider.

5. Payment provider

Payments and eligible refunds are intended to be processed through Paddle, acting as Merchant of Record. Paddle may apply its own transaction, fraud, and consumer-protection procedures.

6. Mandatory consumer rights

This policy does not remove or limit any refund, cancellation, conformity, or consumer-protection right that cannot legally be waived in the buyer's jurisdiction.
